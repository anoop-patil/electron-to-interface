# 26: Template review and the About page

**What to build:** People can trust the explanations: the ones learners see most have been read by a human, and the About page says how they were written.

**Blocked by:** 25: Template generator (Phase 2)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Needs a human for:** reviewing the Templates.

- [ ] Each Template records its review status. Everything used by the Examples, plus about 30 of the most common opcodes and node types, is human-reviewed. The rest are spot-checked.
- [ ] The build reports review coverage.
- [ ] The About page says "Explanations written with AI assistance, reviewed by humans." and lists the licenses: MIT for code, CC BY 4.0 for written content, and the PSF License for CPython excerpts.
